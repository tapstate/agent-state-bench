You are working with six databases to solve this query. They are the operational systems of one retail bank in India. Amounts are in Indian rupees (INR).

Here are the descriptions of these six databases:

1. core_banking
   - This database is stored in a PostgreSQL database and is the bank's core banking system: customers, their deposit accounts, account transactions, cheques, and fixed and recurring deposits.
   - This database consists of six tables:
     - customers: Bank customers
       - Fields: customer_id, name, age, gender, senior_citizen, kyc_status, monthly_income, comm_line1, comm_line2, comm_city, comm_state, comm_pincode, perm_line1, perm_line2, perm_city, perm_state, perm_pincode (comm_ = communication address, perm_ = permanent address)
     - accounts: Savings and current accounts
       - Fields: account_id, customer_id, account_type, status, ifsc, home_branch, nominee_name, nominee_relationship, open_date, available_balance, hold_amount, currency
     - account_holders: Holders of each account, in order (holder_seq 1 is the primary holder)
       - Fields: account_id, holder_seq, holder_name
     - transactions: Account transactions
       - Fields: reference_id, account_id, txn_date, description, amount, direction, channel, balance_after
     - cheques: Cheque leaves issued on accounts
       - Fields: account_id, cheque_number, status
     - deposits: Fixed deposits (kind FD) and recurring deposits (kind RD)
       - Fields: deposit_id, payout_account, kind, principal, rate, tenure_months, start_date, maturity_date, maturity_amount, payout_type, maturity_instruction, status, nominee_name, nominee_relationship, monthly_installment, installments_paid, total_deposited, source_account

2. cards
   - This database is stored in a SQLite database and is the bank's card management system.
   - This database consists of two tables:
     - cards: Debit and credit cards
       - Fields: card_id, account_no, card_type, network, status, credit_limit, available_limit
     - card_controls: Per-card usage controls, one row per channel (atm, online, pos) and region (domestic, international)
       - Fields: card_id, account_no, channel, region, enabled, daily_limit

3. lending
   - This database is stored in a DuckDB database and is the bank's loan management system.
   - This database consists of one table:
     - loans: Home, personal and gold loans
       - Fields: loan_id, borrower_ref, loan_type, outstanding, rate, emi, remaining_tenure_months, next_due_date, status

4. payments
   - This database is stored in a SQLite database and is the bank's payments hub.
   - This database consists of one table:
     - mandates: Standing payment mandates (UPI autopay, NACH and similar) that debit an account
       - Fields: mandate_id, debit_account, payee, amount, frequency, next_debit_date, status, mandate_type

5. crm
   - This database is stored in a MongoDB database and is the bank's customer relationship management system.
   - This database consists of three collections:
     - customers: Customer relationship data
       - Fields: _id, segment, profession, sector, employer, marital_status, email, phone
     - service_requests: Service requests raised by customers
       - Fields: _id, request_id, category, description, related_transaction_id, account_id, status, priority, created_at, eta
     - offers: Product offers shown to customers
       - Fields: _id, customer, offer_id, type, title, detail, indicative

6. insurance
   - This database is stored in a DuckDB database and is the feed of insurance policies the bank distributes for its partner insurers.
   - This database consists of one table:
     - policies: Insurance policies held by bank customers
       - Fields: policy_id, policyholder_ref, policy_name, insurer, sum_assured, premium, frequency, status, next_premium_date, nominee_name, nominee_relationship
