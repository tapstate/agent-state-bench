You are working with six databases to solve this query. They are the operational systems of one bicycle manufacturer, Adventure Works Cycles. Amounts are in US dollars.

Here are the descriptions of these six databases:

1. erp
   - This database is stored in a PostgreSQL database and is the company's ERP sales system: sales territories, stores, customers and sales orders.
   - This database consists of five tables:
     - territories: Sales territories
       - Fields: territory_id, name, country_region_code, territory_group
     - stores: Retail stores that buy from the company
       - Fields: store_id, name, sales_person_id
     - customers: Customers (a store, or an individual buyer when store_id is empty)
       - Fields: customer_id, account_number, store_id, territory_id
     - sales_orders: Sales order headers
       - Fields: sales_order_id, revision_number, order_date, due_date, ship_date, status, online_order_flag, sales_order_number, purchase_order_number, account_number, sales_person_id, customer_id, territory_id, ship_method_id, sub_total, tax_amt, freight, total_due
     - sales_order_lines: Sales order lines
       - Fields: sales_order_id, line_id, sku, carrier_tracking_number, order_qty, special_offer_id, unit_price, unit_price_discount, line_total

2. plm
   - This database is stored in a SQLite database and is the company's product lifecycle management system: the product master and bills of materials.
   - This database consists of five tables:
     - products: Products, both sold products and the components and materials they are built from
       - Fields: product_number, name, make_flag, finished_goods_flag, color, safety_stock_level, reorder_point, size, size_unit_measure_code, weight_unit_measure_code, weight, days_to_manufacture, product_line, class, style, subcategory_id, model_id, sell_start_date, sell_end_date, discontinued_date
     - subcategories: Product subcategories
       - Fields: subcategory_id, category_id, name
     - categories: Product categories
       - Fields: category_id, name
     - models: Product models
       - Fields: model_id, name
     - bom: Bill of materials: the components of each assembly, with the dates each entry applies
       - Fields: bom_id, assembly_number, component_number, start_date, end_date, unit_measure_code, bom_level, per_assembly_qty

3. mes
   - This database is stored in a DuckDB database and is the company's manufacturing execution system.
   - This database consists of four tables:
     - work_orders: Production work orders
       - Fields: work_order_id, part_no, order_qty, stocked_qty, scrapped_qty, start_date, end_date, due_date, scrap_reason_id
     - routings: The operations of each work order, one row per operation, with the work center it ran at
       - Fields: work_order_id, part_no, operation_sequence, work_center_id, scheduled_start_date, scheduled_end_date, actual_start_date, actual_end_date, actual_resource_hrs, planned_cost, actual_cost
     - work_centers: Work centers on the shop floor
       - Fields: work_center_id, name, cost_rate, availability
     - scrap_reasons: Reasons a work order scrapped units
       - Fields: scrap_reason_id, name

4. wms
   - This database is stored in a MongoDB database and is the company's warehouse management system.
   - This database consists of two collections:
     - stock: Quantity on hand per item and location
       - Fields: _id, item, location_id, shelf, bin, quantity
     - locations: Warehouse and shop-floor locations
       - Fields: _id (the location id), name, cost_rate, availability

5. procurement
   - This database is stored in a DuckDB database and is the company's purchasing system.
   - This database consists of five tables:
     - vendors: Suppliers
       - Fields: vendor_id, account_number, name, credit_rating, preferred_vendor_status, active_flag, purchasing_web_service_url
     - vendor_items: Items each vendor supplies, with lead time and price terms
       - Fields: part_ref, vendor_id, average_lead_time, standard_price, last_receipt_cost, last_receipt_date, min_order_qty, max_order_qty, on_order_qty, unit_measure_code
     - purchase_orders: Purchase order headers
       - Fields: purchase_order_id, revision_number, status, employee_id, vendor_id, ship_method_id, order_date, ship_date, sub_total, tax_amt, freight, total_due
     - po_lines: Purchase order lines
       - Fields: purchase_order_id, po_line_id, part_ref, due_date, order_qty, unit_price, line_total, received_qty, rejected_qty, stocked_qty
     - ship_methods: Shipping methods
       - Fields: ship_method_id, name, ship_base, ship_rate

6. costing
   - This database is stored in a PostgreSQL database and is the company's product costing system.
   - This database consists of three tables:
     - standard_cost_history: Standard cost of a product over time; end_date is empty for the period still in effect
       - Fields: product_number, start_date, end_date, standard_cost
     - list_price_history: List price of a product over time; end_date is empty for the period still in effect
       - Fields: product_number, start_date, end_date, list_price
     - item_prices: Current standard cost and list price of every product
       - Fields: product_number, standard_cost, list_price
